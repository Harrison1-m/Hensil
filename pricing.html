Common Inquiries

Do you travel for assignments?

Yes. Our agency accepts international commissions. Travel logistics, destination lodging, and daily project bounds are custom-quoted during your initial design consultation.

What is your payment structure?

We require a non-refundable retainer fee equal to fifty percent of your chosen collection cost to reserve your production date. The remaining percentage scales balance due seven days before execution day.

Can I purchase extra retouching masters?

Absolutely. If your session yields more frames than your baseline package specifies, individual editorial assets can be added into production loop processing at a standard baseline rate of forty-five dollars per file.

How long do you host online galleries?

All finalized luxury cloud galleries are guaranteed private access availability for exactly twelve months following deployment. We advise creating secondary personal archival storage upon receipt.
