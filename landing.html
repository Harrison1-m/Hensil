Common Inquiries

What is your payment structure?

We require a non-refundable retainer fee equal to fifty percent of your chosen collection cost to reserve your production date. The remaining balance is due seven days prior to execution.

How do I receive my invoice?

Upon submitting your booking request through our secure portal, your official studio invoice is instantly generated for immediate review and download.

Do you travel for international assignments?

Yes. Our agency regularly accepts international commissions. Travel logistics and destination lodging are custom-quoted during your initial design consultation.

How long is cloud gallery hosting?

All finalized luxury cloud galleries are guaranteed private access availability for twelve months following deployment.
